Delete your SofaClub account

You can delete your account and all the data linked to it at any time. Deletion happens straight away and can't be undone.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open SofaClub and sign in.
  2. Tap your initial at the top of Today to open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you can't use the app, email {{EMAIL}} from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We'll delete it within one month and confirm by email.

What's deleted

  • your account, name and email address
  • your ratings and watchlist
  • your comments
  • your friend connections, both ways, and your invite code
  • your notifications
  • titles you added yourself
  • your anonymous votes in Loved by the Club

Copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for up to 7 days before being overwritten. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
