5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers. They process data on our instructions and under contract, and can't use it for their own purposes.

RevenueCatChecks subscription purchases with Apple and Google and tells us your planYour account ID (a random code, not your name or email), purchase and subscription records, device and app details

Apple and Google take your subscription payment. They do this as independent companies under their own privacy policies, not on our behalf. We receive your subscription status from them via RevenueCat, never your payment details.

When you search for a title, our server looks it up on public databases (Wikidata, Wikipedia and MusicBrainz). Only the search words are sent, never anything that identifies you.

We may also share data if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of selling or reorganising the business. In a sale, the new owner would have to keep to this policy.

We don't share or sell your data to advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.

6. Where your data is stored

Our database is hosted in London, in the UK. RevenueCat processes subscription data in the United States, and Supabase's support and operations teams may access data from outside the UK. When data leaves the UK, we protect it with the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. You can ask us for details of these safeguards.

7. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. Deletion happens straight away, and removes:

  • your account, name and email address
  • your ratings and watchlist
  • your comments
  • your friend connections, both ways, and your invite code
  • your notifications
  • titles you added yourself
  • your anonymous votes in Loved by the Club

Copies may stay in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for up to 7 days before being overwritten. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies and legal obligations. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first.

If you ask us to delete your data without using the app, we'll do it within one month.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (access)
  • correct anything that's wrong. You can change your name in the app yourself
  • delete your data. You can do this in the app yourself
  • send your data to you or another service in a common format (portability)
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to uses based on legitimate interests

Email {{EMAIL}} to use any of these. We'll reply within one month, and it's free in most cases. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, or 0303 123 1113.

9. Children, security and changes

Children. You must be at least 13 to create an account. If you're under 18, please check with a parent or guardian before signing up. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we'll delete it. Parents can contact us at {{EMAIL}}.

Security. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Passwords are stored only as scrambled hashes. Access rules on our servers decide who can see each person's data, so changing the app can't get around them. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the ICO where the law requires.

Changes. If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
